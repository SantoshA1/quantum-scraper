# E3 pre-registration — generator search round 1 (2026-08-31, WRITTEN BEFORE ANY SIMULATION)

This document is committed before a single generator result is computed. Nothing in
it may be edited after results exist; a failed-but-close generator goes to a future
pre-registered round on NEW data, never to a parameter tweak in this round.

## Shared frame (identical to E2, parity-proven vs lib/analysis/exit_grid.js 25/25)

- Data: `quantum.scorer_bars_daily` (574 symbols, 2026-04-14 → 2026-08-24, OHLCV).
- Signals computable from bars ≤ d only (no lookahead; gap rules may use day-d open
  and prior closes — all known by day-d close). Entry at day-d close. Policy: the
  ratified cell — 2.5% stop (gap-at-open at open, stop-first intraday), exit close
  of d+2. R = ((exit/entry)−1)/0.025.
- Signal window: d0 ∈ [2026-05-13, 2026-08-20] (20-bar warmup; full exit coverage).
- Dedup: one signal per (generator, symbol, d0). Daily cap 5 per generator (ranked
  by each generator's own strength key) — bounds clustering, mimics deployable
  throughput. Overlapping holds allowed: this measures SELECTION quality, not
  portfolio construction.
- Nulls, 1:1 matched per signal, deterministic md5 seeds (same recipe as E2):
  N1 = random symbol, same day, from the simable universe; N2 = same symbol,
  random simable day.
- Inference: paired day-block bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, seed 20260831.

## Generator specs (parameters FROZEN here; no sweeps, no variants this round)

**G1 — momentum continuation (long).** Signal on day d iff:
c_d ≥ 1.10 × c_{d−20} (20-bar momentum ≥ +10%), AND c_d > c_{d−5}, AND
c_d ≥ 0.98 × max(h over d−19..d) (within 2% of 20-bar high).
Strength key (daily cap ranking): 20-bar momentum, descending.

**G2 — gap-and-go (long).** Signal on day d iff:
o_d ≥ 1.02 × c_{d−1} (gap up ≥ 2%), AND c_d > o_d (held the gap into the close).
Strength key: gap size o_d/c_{d−1}, descending.

**G3 — mean-reversion in uptrend (long).** Signal on day d iff:
c_d ≤ 0.96 × c_{d−1} (down ≥ 4% on the day), AND c_d ≥ 1.00 × c_{d−20}
(20-bar momentum still non-negative), AND c_d ≥ 0.85 × sma20_d (not collapsed).
Strength key: day drop c_d/c_{d−1}, ascending (biggest dip first).

## Graduation rule (frozen before results)

A generator graduates to a paper proposal ONLY if ALL hold:
1. Δ mean R > 0 vs BOTH N1 and N2 (point estimates);
2. bootstrap p(Δ≤0) ≤ 0.0167 vs EACH null (Bonferroni for 3 generators);
3. mean R ≥ +0.15 (economic bar: survives a fees/slippage haircut ≈ 0.05–0.10R);
4. n ≥ 60 signals (else "insufficient sample," parked for the data to grow).

Anything less is reported honestly as a fail or as underpowered — with NO
same-round modification. Round 2, if wanted, pre-registers new specs and runs
when the bars store has grown (backfill or fresh accumulation).

## Known limitations, stated up front

One ~70-day signal window in a single chop-up regime; daily bars (no intraday
timing); fees/slippage excluded symmetrically (hence the +0.15 economic bar);
574-symbol universe fixed by the existing bars store; three specs chosen by prior
literature-shaped judgment, not by peeking at this data.
