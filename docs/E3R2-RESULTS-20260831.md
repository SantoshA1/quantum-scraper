## What this evidence now says, plainly

At this granularity — daily bars, ~590 liquid names, close entries, a 2-day
horizon — the market looks efficient against everything tried. The one recurring
positive (momentum-symbol drift) is real but thin, decays out of window, and no
timing overlay improves on simply holding those names. Two research axes remain
genuinely untested and are the rational next probes, in order: (1) **the horizon
axis** — if leader drift compounds, 5–10 day holds on the same inventory may clear
the economic bar where 2-day scratches cannot (round 3, same harness, cheap);
(2) **the liquidity/volume axis** (the `v` column is so far unused). If those fail
too, the honest conclusion is that this data granularity does not pay, and the
human-in-the-loop product (option B) becomes the strongest branch — supported by
the one green bucket in live history.
