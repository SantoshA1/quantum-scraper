## Hypothesis under test (from round 1's G1 decomposition)

Momentum identifies good inventory (+0.34 mean R on the same symbols at random
times) but entry AT the highs is a tax (−0.40R, CI fully negative). Prediction:
buying momentum LEADERS on PULLBACK days recovers the inventory quality without
paying the timing tax.

## Frame

- Data: `quantum.scorer_bars_daily` post-backfill (146,797 rows, 2025-09-02 →
  2026-08-28, 250 days, 591 symbols, feed=iex).
- **Signal window: d0 ∈ [2025-10-06, 2026-04-09]** — 20-bar warmup from 09-02;
  exits complete by 2026-04-13; zero overlap with the hypothesis-generating window.
- Sim, entry, policy, dedup, 5/day cap, nulls (N1 rand-sym same day, N2 same-sym
  rand day, seeds md5(gen|sym|d0|salt), salts e3n1/e3n2), and paired day-block
  bootstrap (2,000 resamples, salt `e3r2boot`): identical to round 1 / E2.

## Specs (FROZEN)

**G4 — leader pullback.** Signal day d iff: c_d ≥ 1.10 × c_{d−20} (leader), AND
c_d < c_{d−1} (down-close pullback), AND c_d ≥ 0.90 × max(h over d−19..d) (trend
intact). Strength key: 20-bar momentum desc.

**G5 — leader deeper pullback.** As G4, but the pullback condition is
c_d ≤ 0.97 × max(h over d−5..d−1) (≥3% below the prior-5-bar high) instead of a
mere down-close. Strength: 20-bar momentum desc.

**G6 — control: pullback WITHOUT leadership.** c_d < c_{d−1} AND
c_d ≥ 0.90 × max(h over d−19..d), NO momentum condition. Strength: closeness to
the 20-bar high (c_d / h20) desc. Purpose: isolates whether the LEADER condition
adds value beyond generic dip-near-highs. G6 is a control — it reports, and its
comparison to G4 is a secondary analysis; it graduates only under the same rule
as any spec.

## Graduation rule (identical to round 1, FROZEN)

Δ mean R > 0 vs BOTH nulls; bootstrap p(Δ≤0) ≤ 0.0167 vs each (Bonferroni, 3
specs); mean R ≥ +0.15; n ≥ 60. Secondary (reported, non-gating): G4 − G6 delta,
to judge the leader condition itself. A graduate advances to a PAPER PROPOSAL for
the Conclave — never directly to trading.

## Limitations

Single 6-month disjoint window (still one broad regime era); daily bars; IEX feed
(occasional thin-symbol gaps); fees excluded symmetrically (hence the +0.15 bar);
specs shaped by one prior decomposition — a graduate here still needs its
Conclave-defined forward confirmation before any capital, paper included.
