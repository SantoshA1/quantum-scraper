## The one-paragraph situation

QTP has been entry-frozen since 08-19. The freeze is correct behavior: the certified long
book measures **PF 0.6585 over n=27** and the gate refuses to trade a measured-negative

## DECISION 1 — Adopt the long-side exit policy: **2.5% fixed stop + hard 2-day time exit**

**The evidence (pre-committed bar: PF > 1.2 on BOTH cohorts, n≥20/30):**

| run | fresh data added | A_long (executed) | B_blocked (never traded) | clears? |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Decay is flattening (1.46 → 1.37 → 1.35): convergence, not erosion. Robust across every
percentage stop (1.0–3.0% all clear all three runs); the loose family (no-stop, 2.5×ATR)
failed OOS both times — the tight-stop shape is load-bearing, not tuned. Independently
cross-validated (RCF scorer measured the same population at 1.40 vs this engine's 1.38).
The Gate-K-blocked cohort itself (n=139 mature) runs PF 1.03–1.11 under this policy —
the gate's rejects are breakeven-plus at 2-day holds: **the exit determines the sign.**
Chosen cell at 08-25: win rate 50%, stop rate 32%, expectancy **+0.38%/trade** (blocked
cohort) and +0.63%/trade (executed cohort).

**What changes on ratification (contract level; byte-level patches follow with suites):**
- **Stop width:** 2.5% fixed, GTC, from entry — replaces the ~1.2% regime. Three sites:
  the executor's bracket construction, the Gate-K Prep stop-parity clamp (the gate must
  judge the stop the order will carry), and the TSM: **for policy-cohort trades the TSM
  monitors but does not tighten** — E1 measured a fixed stop, and the probation cohort
  runs the measured policy, not an untested hybrid.
- **Time exit (new mechanism):** a small scheduled workflow, weekdays 15:50 ET — close
  any policy-cohort position at the close of its **2nd bar after entry day** (engine
  day-indexing: enter Tuesday → exit Thursday 15:50). Market order, fail-loud, audited.
- The earnings guard (gov 235) already covers the entry side; unchanged.

**Risk & rollback:** worst structural case per trade ≈ −1R = 0.5% of equity ≈ **$500**
at probation size; overnight-gap tail is the known p99 3.06% (~1.2R) with the earnings
class now guarded. Rollback = revert the three patches + disable the exit workflow; the
old policy is preserved in version history.
