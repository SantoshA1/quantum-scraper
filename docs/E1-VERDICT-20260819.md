## Honesty box — read before acting

1. **Day-0 convention.** The engine evaluates from the first bar AFTER entry day; there is
   no same-day intraday stop. Day-0 adverse moves ARE priced (they land at day-1's open,
   gap-throughs filled at the open), but the tested policy implicitly means "disaster stop
   only on entry day." The live book's PF 0.9755 is therefore not apples-to-apples with the
   grid — the grid tests a genuinely different policy, which is the point.
2. **Sample weights.** B (n≈324) is the load-bearing cohort; A_long (n=21) supports
   directionally. The 13-cell robustness across stop widths — not one tuned cell — is the
   defense against data-mining, plus the out-of-implementation cross-check above.
3. **One tape.** Jun–Aug 2026, CHOP/VIX-24. The policy is certified for THIS market
   character only; it must re-verify as regimes change (the runner re-runs idempotently
   under new run_ids).
4. **Costs.** Returns are unsized per-trade %; slippage/fees not modeled. At +0.5–0.6%
   expectancy per trade (B, 2-day cells), realistic slippage matters and must be measured
   in shadow before real money.
5. This is paper-cohort research. Nothing in this document changes live behavior.
