## The two findings

1. **Momentum selection adds almost nothing beyond universe exposure — and
   concentrates risk brutally.** The plain equal-weight basket of all ~570
   watchlist names matched P1's return within noise while running one QUARTER of
   its drawdown and twice its Sharpe. At K=10, concentration noise dominates
   selection skill: one in seven purely random 10-name baskets beat the momentum
   ranker. The symbol-level drift the E3 rounds kept detecting is real but it is
   mostly UNIVERSE drift, not rankable edge.
2. **The universe's +34% vs SPY's +14% carries the declared survivorship caveat**
   (today's watchlist applied historically). Relative comparisons within the
   universe are clean; the absolute universe-vs-SPY gap is not a deployable claim.

## The four-round program, concluded

E2: the live funnel's entries are indistinguishable from darts (n=904).
E3 r1–r3: ten entry constructions — the day never mattered; only symbols did;
stops only taxed the drift. E4: harvesting that drift as a momentum portfolio
fails against its own universe risk-adjusted. **Twelve constructions, four
pre-registered rounds, zero graduates.** In this data — daily bars, liquid names,
~11 months — there is no deployable edge at any construction tried, and the
honest systematic alternative (broad exposure) is not a product QTP needs to build.
