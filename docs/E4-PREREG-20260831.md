# E4 pre-registration — continuous momentum portfolio (2026-08-31, BEFORE ANY SIMULATION)

Committed before results exist; frozen thereafter. This is the convergent test the
E2/E3 program points to: harvest the symbol-level drift as a CONTINUOUS PORTFOLIO
(no triggers, no stops), which also dissolves the random-day critique — a
continuous portfolio has no entry day.

## Strategy cells (2, FROZEN)

At each rebalance close, rank all eligible symbols (≥21 trailing bars) by 20-bar
momentum c_d / c_{d−20}, using ONLY trailing data; hold the TOP 10 equal-weight
until the next rebalance.
- **P1**: rebalance every 5 sessions. **P2**: rebalance every 10 sessions.
First rebalance 2025-10-06; window ends 2026-08-28. Equal weight is reset daily
within holding periods (declared approximation, benign at K=10 over ≤10 days).

## Benchmarks and nulls

- **NB3 universe**: equal-weight ALL eligible symbols, same mechanics — separates
  momentum SELECTION from universe drift. **NB2**: SPY buy-and-hold.
- **NB1 random baskets**: 100 independent paths; each picks 10 UNIFORM-RANDOM
  eligible symbols at every P1 rebalance date (deterministic md5 seeds, salt
  `e4rand`), same costs. The strategy's Monte-Carlo p = share of paths with
  terminal return ≥ the cell's.

## Costs (frozen)

5 bps per side per name traded → 10 bps round trip on turnover; a full 10-name
swap at a rebalance costs 0.10% of book. Applied to P1, P2, and every NB1 path
(NB3 turnover ~0; SPY costless).

## Graduation rule (frozen; a graduate goes to a Conclave PAPER proposal only)

ALL must hold per cell, net of costs:
1. total return > NB3 (universe) AND > SPY;
2. ≥97 of the 100 random paths finish below it (Monte-Carlo p ≤ 0.03; Bonferroni
   for 2 cells);
3. economic bar: annualized return ≥ SPY + 3 percentage points;
4. max drawdown ≤ 1.5 × SPY's max drawdown over the window;
5. ≥200 trading days simulated.

## Declared limitations

The 591-symbol store is today's watchlist applied historically (mild survivorship
— it favors names that were interesting recently; NB1/NB3 share the same universe,
so RELATIVE comparisons are clean, and the SPY comparison carries the survivorship
caveat). Single ~11-month window; daily close fills; IEX bars. Momentum-parameter
(20-bar, K=10) chosen from the E3 lineage, not swept — no same-round variants.
